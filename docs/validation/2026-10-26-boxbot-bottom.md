Validated on 2026-10-26 using Claude Code 2.1.308 on macOS ARM64.

## Result

The conveyor occupies the bottom of the docked pane. Flow occupies the top of the shared pane, leaving free space between the two sections. BoxBot alone leaves free space above the conveyor. No controls were added.

The layout uses `e.props.scroll.bodyRows`, the native pane's usable height, rather than the whole terminal's height. A resize remeasures that value. Flow owns one shared frame, and BoxBot fills its footer in either hook order. Empty footer children and the original opaque host drawing are covered by regression tests.

## Limits

Claude controls whether a pane is docked or inline. Inline panes remain compact. If the combined content exceeds the available height, Claude scrolls the entire pane; BoxBot is not an independent sticky scrolling region. Shared bottom anchoring requires Flow 0.1.1 and BoxBot 0.3.1 or later. Restart Claude after updating both installed mods.
